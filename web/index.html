Executive overview

Compare fraud coverage, investigation workload and illustrative financial value.

Historical demonstration using 2013 card payments. This is an example of review allocation, not a live fraud control.

What the results show

The dataset does not specify a currency. Financial value uses its payment amounts and the assumed review costs and loss reduction; it is not verified money saved.

The decision to make

Set the balance between finding more fraud cases, avoiding unnecessary customer reviews, and focusing investigations on larger potential losses.

These results use the last applied business assumptions. Change them in Review options to see how the balance shifts. Actual operating costs and intervention results would need to be measured before a business rollout.

Supporting model validation — for specialist review

The model estimates fraud likelihood. It was chosen using an earlier validation period and evaluated on a later, separate test period.

Precision and recall

Precision measures fraud among flagged payments. Recall measures the share of fraud found.

Probability calibration

Predicted probabilities compared with observed fraud rates.

Ten equally sized groups. Confidence intervals are not available.

How financial value is calculated
The fraud-risk option reviews the payments most likely to be fraudulent. The financial-impact option also considers payment size, review costs and assumed loss reduction, and only selects reviews with positive estimated value.
Expected benefit = probability × amount × effectiveness − review cost − (1 − probability) × false-review cost
Reported benefit uses the known test labels and assumed effectiveness and costs. It is not measured prevented loss.

About this source
CFPB Consumer Complaint Database, filtered to “Money transfer, virtual currency, or money service”. Issue and sub-issue categories match fraud, scam, scammed, unauthorised/unauthorized or identity theft. No narratives are used.
Complaints are self-selected, can be revised, and do not measure population fraud rates or explain card-model performance.
