01 / MODEL EVIDENCE

From rare events
to measurable performance.

Time-separated evaluation of anonymised European card transactions from September 2013.

Discrimination & calibration

Average precision measures ranking; Brier score measures probability error (lower is better). Calibration bins contain equal numbers of transactions. Few positive labels make estimates uncertain; confidence intervals are not available.

Candidate selection evidence

These metrics are from the earlier selection period, not the final test period.

Chronology and provenance
Elapsed seconds are not calendar timestamps. Anonymous PCA features and log amount are used for modelling. Weekly rebuilds reuse the fixed 2013 dataset.

03 / INDEPENDENT PUBLIC TRENDS
Listen to the
complaint signals.
Published US money-transfer complaints, grouped by received date.
These complaints are separate from the European card model. Category matches are not confirmed fraud and are not fraud prevalence.
Complaints over time
Recent days may be incomplete due to publication delays. Counts can be revised. Missing dates appear as gaps, not assumed zeros.
Source and interpretation
CFPB Consumer Complaint Database, filtered to “Money transfer, virtual currency, or money service”. Issue/sub-issue categories match fraud, scam, scammed, unauthorised/unauthorized or identity theft. No narratives are used. Complaints are self-selected and cannot establish population fraud rates or explain card-model performance.
